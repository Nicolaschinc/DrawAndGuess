# DrawAndGuess Sitewide SEO Expansion

## Goal

Expand SEO from a single homepage into a small set of focused landing pages that map to distinct search intents:

- homepage: broad product intent
- features page: capability intent
- use-cases page: scenario intent
- faq page: question intent

## Why This Structure

The homepage should stay conversion-oriented. If every search intent is forced onto one page, the page gets crowded and weaker for both users and crawlers.

These support pages let the site target adjacent intents without weakening the create/join funnel on the homepage.

## Page Roles

### `/features`
- explain what the product currently supports
- reinforce concrete capability claims
- support searches from users comparing browser party games

### `/use-cases`
- map the product to common group scenarios
- support long-tail intent around remote team games, friend hangouts, or classroom use

### `/faq`
- answer pre-click and pre-share objections
- align visible FAQ content with FAQ structured data
- capture question-style searches

## Internal Linking Strategy

- homepage links to all three support pages
- footer links to all major static pages
- sitemap includes all localized URLs
- prerender ensures these pages are crawlable without relying on client-side rendering

## Success Metrics After Launch

- indexed page count for new landing pages
- impressions and clicks for non-homepage queries
- CTR by page and query cluster
- engagement quality on organic sessions
- conversion from organic landing page visit to room creation or room join

## What To Watch

- if `/features` gets impressions but poor CTR, title/description may be too generic
- if `/faq` gets indexed but no clicks, the questions may not match real search demand
- if homepage still gets almost all organic impressions, internal linking and topic separation may still be too weak
